    Stay safe from job scams

    Never pay to get hired

    Real employers don't charge for interviews, training, kits or registration.

    Verify the company

    Find it on its official website and LinkedIn. Call the number listed there.

    Check the email domain

    Offers from free addresses like Gmail or Yahoo are a warning sign.

    Expect a real interview

    Instant joining with no interview is how most scams start.

    Guard your documents

    Don't share Aadhaar, PAN, bank details or OTPs before a signed offer letter.

    Doubt easy money

    Very high pay for simple work from home is rarely real.

    Already paid or shared your details?

    In India, call the National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 right away and report at cybercrime.gov.in. Also tell your bank.
